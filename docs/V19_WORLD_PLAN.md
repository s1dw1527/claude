# v19 World Visual Overhaul: inspection and plan

This file comes first, as the brief asks: what exists, which scripts build it, what changes, and how to roll back.

## How the map is built today
Everything is generated by **server scripts at startup**. There is no hand-placed map in the place file. That makes every change reviewable and testable, and a rollback is just restoring the previous scripts.

| Area | Built by | What it looks like today |

## The main problem
The two-thirds of the city made of district slabs is **empty gray ground** until players buy plots, and the Downtown skyline is six identical boxes. Adding neon and particles wouldn't fix that. What fixes it is buildings with identity, so that's where the work goes.

## Plan (each phase committed and tested on its own)
1. **Architecture kit** (new `GameServer/Architecture.lua`): data-driven building styles in one table, `C.ARCH_STYLES`. Each style sets materials, colors, roof, windows, awning, sign and extras. Windows light up warm at night (a single server switch at dusk and dawn, not per-frame).
2. **Unsold plots get real buildings.** Each district gets a vacant building in its own style (Midtown restaurants, Entertainment neon, Suburbs small shops, Expansion half-built construction, Downtown glass, Industrial sheds, Waterfront beach houses), with a "FOR SALE" banner. The existing sign and its *View Property* / *Buy* prompt stay at the front of the plot, reachable. Buying it replaces it with the owner's business, exactly as today.
3. **Downtown skyline:** the 6 towers rebuilt with different silhouettes (setbacks, crowns, spires, lit window bands), plus a central fountain plaza. The lots, the Grand Hotel and the reserved footprints stay.
4. **Restaurant Row** is Midtown, the equivalent area: the main business district, with bakery and coffee boosts. No duplicate district. It gets string lights over its streets, outdoor seating and umbrellas, menu boards, and the gate reads "MIDTOWN • RESTAURANT ROW".
5. **Shopping Mall:** one recognizable landmark with a parking lot. It's placed on free land and checked against roads, plots and reserved paths.
6. **Beachfront:** a boardwalk, umbrellas and loungers, a lighthouse at the end of the pier, and snack stands.
7. **Industrial:** loading docks, pipes, catwalks, parked trucks, warning stripes.
8. **Landmarks check:** the theater (marquee and searchlights), Raceway, Fun Park and mountain base already have their set pieces. They only get touch-ups where something is missing.
9. **Logo:** a crown "CORNER EMPIRE" with "BUILD • RUN • EXPLORE • RISE" on the menu and title card.
10. **Tests:**
    - a new `world_test`: every district has buildings, unsold plots keep a reachable buy prompt, night windows switch on and off, the part budget holds;
    - `layout_test` (nothing on any road), `terrain_test`, `perf_test`, `mobile_test`;
    - the full regression, build, report and Studio checklist.

## Rules held throughout
- DataStore `CornerEmpire_v5` untouched, and **no save changes**: this is visual only.
- No system is removed. Lots, prompts, deeds, the theater, park building, heists and races keep working.
- No external or paid assets: only parts, terrain, materials, lights and particles.
- Part budget: the world is about 18.5k visible parts today. The target is under 25k, with the final count reported. Effects are local and bounded.

## Backup and rollback
- **Backup:** git. The last shipped state is commit `18a460c` (v18), and `CornerEmpire_v18.rbxlx` stays in the repo.
- **Rollback:** reinstall the v18 drop-ins (`git show 18a460c:updates/GameServer.rbxmx`) or open `CornerEmpire_v18.rbxlx`. No save data is involved.
